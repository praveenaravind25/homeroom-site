A recipe shared from a phone lands in the recipe box on the wall. On the weekend the household plans the week right there: a dish or two across the lunches and dinners, with days and portions, and the plan becomes one shopping list, grouped by aisle, that Lock in sends to both phones.
At the stove, Cook it gives the whole wall to one step at a time, with a silent clip of that step on a loop and just the quantities it needs.
Start the 12 minute simmer, move on to the cucumbers, and it keeps counting; when it's done, the wall says so out loud, once.
