Before it could be a calendar, it had to be a good photo frame: it balances people and years, skips screenshots and blurry shots, and a long press on any photo says, in plain words, why it was chosen.
Now, on a day with history, the wall tells one short story a day, after 6 am: a few photos from one past year under a title, "3 years ago today", that settles in on the first photo, then shrinks to the corner and stays put while the photos change.
If any of them can't load, there's no story at all and the wall carries on as usual, so nothing ever looks broken.
The Designer offered three ways to tell it, and the recommended one shipped.
