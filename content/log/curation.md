Before it could be a calendar, it had to be a good photo frame.
Curation balances people and years, skips screenshots, receipts and blurry shots, and can say in plain words why it picked a photo.
Photos fill the screen edge to edge with a slow drift, and the wall moves between Ambient, Glance and Night instead of sitting in one state.
