Before it could be a calendar, it had to be a good photo frame: it balances people and years, skips screenshots and blurry shots, and lets each photo drift slowly, edge to edge.
Now a long press on the photo asks why it was chosen, and the answer comes back in plain words.
Like nudges that photo to come round again, Hide lets it go for good (with an Undo), and a tap still opens the Rail.
The owner picked the Designer's quietest direction: no box or card, just the photo's own corner, saying why.
