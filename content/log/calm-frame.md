The slow pan across every photo is gone: each one now gets a single still crop, chosen so the faces sit above the clock instead of under it, and a photo that arrives sideways is turned upright or left out.
The date shows once, and the clock and weather no longer hop when a new photo settles in.
Lists, recipes and sheets on the wall now scroll under a finger, with the kiosk's own swipe gestures switched off so a drag never leaves the page.
