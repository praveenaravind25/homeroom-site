A quiet gear at the foot of the Rail now opens Settings, so the household can tune the wall without anyone editing a file on the Mac.
Display sets how long the wall waits before going back to photos, and when the screen goes dark at night and wakes again.
A change is kept on the server and the wall picks it up straight away, with no reload; if a save fails, nothing changes and it says so, calmly.
It all goes through one closed, checked write path, so the wall can change its settings and nothing else.
