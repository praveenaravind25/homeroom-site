02 · Dinner, start to finish

Dinner, start to finish: plan the week at the wall, shop one list from your phone, and cook from the wall.

Plan the week at the wall.

A dish or two covers the week's lunches and dinners. Pick them, set the days and portions, and the grid fills in.

One list, by aisle.

The plan becomes one shopping list, scaled to your portions. Identical items merge, and the staples you always have are skipped.

In your pocket at the store.

Lock in the week and the list is on everyone's phone. Tick things off in the aisle, and the wall knows.

Then the wall cooks with you.

Cook it hands the whole wall to one step at a time: that step's clip on a loop, just the quantities it needs.

One step. The whole wall.

Readable from the stove, with timers that keep counting while you move on.
