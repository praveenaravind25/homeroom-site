Homeroom: a wall tablet that's a photo frame first, until you touch it.

01 · The Wall

It's a photo frame.

Your photos, a quiet clock, the weather and what's next. Readable from across the kitchen.

02 · A touch

Until you touch it.

A touch means you want something. The photo steps aside and today rises.

03 · Week

The week, in one look.

Everyone has a colour, and what's for dinner sits on every day.

04 · Meals

Plan the week at the wall.

Pick a dish or two from the recipe box, set the days and portions, and the week fills in.

05 · Shopping

One list, in your pocket.

The plan becomes one list by aisle, scaled to your portions. Tick it off at the store, and the wall ticks too.

06 · Cook

Then the wall cooks with you.

One step at a time: that step's clip on a loop, just the quantities it needs, and a timer that keeps counting.

07 · Home

The lights, without the app.

Only what you'd reach for. Tap Dinner and the table lights come on.

08 · Idle

Then it leans back.

Leave it alone and the photo comes home.
